# EchoPath competition evidence register

## Source and interpretation

The team supplied its Step 3 Lean Canvas and PRD document, which reports a survey of **8 respondents from one cohort**. The findings below preserve that team's reported evidence. Raw respondent-level survey data was not independently inspected. This is directional problem evidence, not validation results from the enhanced EchoPath application.

| Team-reported survey finding                                    | Result                                         | Product implication                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Knowing which skills/tools matter is the biggest challenge      | 6/8 (75%)                                      | Skills-first discovery and extracted tags                                            |
| Learnings are lost because nobody asks when the experience ends | 5/8 (62.5%)                                    | Opt-in automated capture reminders                                                   |
| No central submission place                                     | 3/8 (37.5%)                                    | One experience hub                                                                   |
| Debriefing takes too long                                       | 2/8 (25%)                                      | Short guided intake; measure actual time                                             |
| NDA worry                                                       | 0/8                                            | Retain privacy/redaction safeguards despite no reported concern in this small sample |
| Willing to share anonymously                                    | 6 yes, 1 maybe, 1 no                           | Potential supply; willingness is not completed publication consent                   |
| Current sources                                                 | LinkedIn 8/8; career services 6/8; ChatGPT 5/8 | Firsthand, program-specific content and synthesized retrieval                        |
| Difficulty finding information                                  | Average approximately 3.4/5                    | Moderate reported pain; broaden testing                                              |
| Usefulness of the proposed platform                             | Average approximately 3.9/5                    | Baseline concept response, not measured satisfaction with this implementation        |

The source also gives ideation impact/feasibility scores as team judgments, not experimentally measured performance. It prioritizes the capture/reminder plus skills-first retrieval loop, defers Q&A, and excludes peer routing from the MVP.

## Proposed success targets not yet demonstrated

- Debrief completion at least 60%.
- Median submission time no more than 5 minutes.
- Usefulness at least 4/5.
- Testers perform at least two searches.
- At least ten seeded debriefs for launch.

No target is claimed as achieved. Browser automation timing is not human completion time. Fictional demo fixtures are not ten real student debriefs. Six survey respondents willing to contribute are potential recruits, not six completed contributions. Local analytics are actual records from this browser, not community-wide usage statistics.

## Engineering changes informed by the supplied findings

The enhancement prioritizes opt-in end-date reminders, a shorter intake, searchable extracted tags, grounded cross-experience synthesis, anonymous publication and contributor control. These are responses to the team-reported Step 3 findings and PRD. They must not be described as a measured improvement from post-build usability testing.
